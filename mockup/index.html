About

Behind the lens

I started with weddings, and they're still the heart of what I do. There's nothing like the trust of being handed someone's most important day and asked to keep it forever.

Over the years that love of real moments grew into portraits, maternity sessions, and the wide quiet of landscape work. Different subjects, same approach: patient, warm, and honest.

I shoot in natural light wherever I can, and I'd rather catch the laugh between the poses than the pose itself.

— LOJ
